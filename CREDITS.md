## Sun Wukong illustration

- **Files:** `public/images/wukong-gold-800.png`, `public/images/wukong-gold-1200.png`
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons — [File:Xiyou.PNG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Xiyou.PNG)
- **Original:** Anonymous 19th-century illustration from *Xiyou Yuanzhi Tuxiang*
  (西遊原旨圖像), a Chinese edition of *Journey to the West*.
- **License:** Public domain (author died more than 70 years ago).
- **Edits:** paper margin trimmed; ink colorized gold and made transparent so it can sit on a dark
  background. This is public-domain mythology, not artwork from *Black Myth: Wukong*.
